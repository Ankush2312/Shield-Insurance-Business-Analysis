# 📌 Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the **AtliQ Technologies Data Analytics Virtual Internship**, where I analyzed the business performance of **Shield Insurance** using **Power BI**.

The objective was not only to build an interactive dashboard but also to perform business analysis by identifying customer behavior, revenue drivers, sales channel performance, and growth opportunities.

The project demonstrates how business intelligence can support data-driven decision-making through effective visualization and storytelling.

---

# 🎯 Business Objectives

This analysis answers the following business questions:

- Which age groups contribute the highest revenue?
- Which cities generate the highest business?
- How does customer value change across age groups?
- Which sales channels contribute the most revenue?
- How are customer acquisition channels evolving?
- What strategies can improve business growth?

# 📈 Key Business Insights

## 👥 Age Group Analysis

- Customers aged **31–40** generated the highest revenue and customer base.
- Average Revenue Per Customer increased steadily with age.
- Older customers preferred broader coverage and higher-value insurance plans.
- Claim settlement rates improved across older age groups.

---

## 🏙 City Analysis

- Delhi NCR generated the highest revenue and customer base.
- Mumbai and Hyderabad followed as the next strongest markets.
- Revenue differences across cities were primarily driven by customer volume rather than customer value.

---

## 💼 Sales Channel Analysis

- Offline Agents contributed more than **55%** of total revenue and customers.
- Online App and Website showed continuous growth during the analysis period.
- Digital channels eventually surpassed Offline Direct, indicating changing customer preferences.
